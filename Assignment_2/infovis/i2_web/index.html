DAS732 A2 - I2: browser visualizations of the A1 Reddit data (D3.js v7)

Owner: Parthsarati Samanta (BT2024083). Serve this folder with python -m http.server 8000 and open http://localhost:8000/.

  • I2a - Parallel coordinates with edge bundling: 2,000 top posts (40 per subreddit), 8 axes. Brush any axis (several brushes combine), drag an axis title to reorder it, use the bundling-strength slider to compare unbundled and bundled views, switch the colour/bundling group, hover a line for details.
  • I2b - Zoomable treemap: 49,266 posts. Choose between three hierarchy remodels, three size measures and five tiling strategies; click to zoom in, click the breadcrumb to zoom out.
